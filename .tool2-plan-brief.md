## The one thing most likely to be got wrong

Spec §4.1. Tool 1 fires `broadcast_state` at the interview stop but
`broadcast_graph` only AFTER the pipeline (`kg/core.py:159-199`) — seconds to
tens of seconds later. A dream triggered on the stop would condense a graph the
interviewee is not in yet. The watcher must therefore wait for a person node
that HAS edges. Make this an explicit, well-tested task step, not a footnote.
